# BANE — the constitution (lineage card)

Bane was the brand-neutral master audit that Reckoning, Justice and UltimaWeapon descend from. Its doctrine
is the constitution of every movement here; its Ahrefs-era data phases (Foundation pull, batch-analysis,
Site Audit telemetry) are **retired in UltimaWeapon**, which never calls a connector — Reckoning's
export-and-fetch spine replaced them. Read this card when you need the original wording of a rule, the
Part II recovery-playbook section list, or the build/verify mechanics. Nothing here licenses an Ahrefs
call.

## The rules Bane wrote that every movement still keeps

- Never invent a number. Every metric traces to a fetched page, a public API, a user export or a
  screenshot. Label evidence observed / inferred / unverified; put data limitations in the report's FIRST
  content section.
- Grades and findings are honest. Name what is already working in every module — an all-red report reads
  as a sales pitch.
- SEO outcomes are never guaranteed; projections are hedged ranges labeled estimates.
- Brand neutrality is the whole point: no company/agency name on cover, header, footer, workbook Summary,
  or metadata; "Prepared for [Client]" only; author/creator metadata blank or the recipient's name (offer
  it); scrub docinfo + XMP with pikepdf; sweep every file (PDF text + raw bytes + metadata, workbook, txt,
  csv) for brand tokens and ship at zero hits.
- Monetary fields from any vendor export may be in cents — sanity-check magnitudes before they touch a
  deliverable.

## Part II — the 90-day recovery playbook (section list, verbatim intent)

(10) phased 90-day plan + a 12-move table · (11) on-page fixes — a drop-in title tag (~60 chars) AND meta
description for EVERY page · (12) ready-to-paste JSON-LD (Organization, Course/Product+Offer with real
price, LocalBusiness/{vertical}, FAQPage) filled with the client's real facts · (13) exact .htaccess 301s
+ self-referencing canonicals for duplicate URLs · (14) the content offensive — a 12-week, 20–24 article
plan (working title, primary term + demand evidence, target money page, word count) with each piece
structured to rank AND be AI-cited (answer in the first two sentences, question-shaped H2s, FAQ schema,
descriptive internal link to the paid page — link targets verified against the live sitemap) · (15)
authority — numbered disavow-submission steps + outreach target groups + two ready-to-send email
templates · (16) AI answers — a full llms.txt draft, the robots.txt AI-bot allow block, a brand-fact
consistency checklist · (17) measurement — analytics events, a KPI baseline table, hedged
30/60/90/180-day milestones. Appendices: keyword matrix, keyword gap, link gap, printable master task
checklist, full disavow list.

## Build and verify mechanics Bane established

- Order: charts → workbook → CSV → report (which quotes the others). Charts in matplotlib with a neutral
  palette; plot NUMERIC x so duplicate month labels do not collapse, and re-apply categorical tick labels
  AFTER any shared styling helper.
- The report is a docx built per the docx skill (US-Letter 12240×15840, DXA dual table widths,
  ShadingType.CLEAR, LevelFormat.BULLET, PageBreak inside a Paragraph, PositionalTab dot leaders, never a
  literal \n), rendered to PDF, TOC page numbers measured with pdftotext and rebuilt.
- Verify by looking: cover, TOC, one table-heavy page, one chart page, one code-block page, the roadmap;
  fix blank pages and orphans; re-verify TOC after any pagination change; cross-check headline numbers
  against raw data; disavow.txt equals the disavow bucket exactly; CSV UTF-8 no BOM with exact headers.
- Deliver with a short summary: top 3 findings, the single biggest competitor threat, data caveats, the
  first-sprint move — and offer to put the recipient's name on the cover and in the metadata.
