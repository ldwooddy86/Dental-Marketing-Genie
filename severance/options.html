The live watch, its notifications and badge, the addresses the provider consoles need, and site access.

Live watch

In the background the extension reads the Texas Workforce Commission's WARN notices for the watched counties (data.texas.gov) and Texas weekly initial claims (FRED), on this timer.

Watched counties

The Live Desk (module 25) sends the firm's counties here when it runs inside the extension; a change made here holds until the desk's county list changes. None picked means all of Texas.

Notifications and badge

A desktop notification for each new WARN notice in the watched counties, with the campaign rule it triggers, and when Texas weekly claims jump past the thresholds. The badge counts new notices in the last 14 days.

Provider consoles

Every OAuth client the firm creates for module 23 (Google Cloud, Meta for Developers, TikTok for Business, Microsoft Azure, LinkedIn) needs this redirect address. In Firefox the address is different for each browser profile (it ends in extensions.allizom.org) and the add-on must be granted site access below before a sign in can finish.
